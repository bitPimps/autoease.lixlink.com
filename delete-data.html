Delete your AutoEase data

AutoEase has no account and no server. Your data is on your phone, and in your own iCloud or Google Drive if you turned on backup. Here’s how to remove it.

Last updated November 14, 2026

Delete everything on your phone

Uninstall AutoEase. That deletes your car, its schedule, service log, fuel log, to-fix notes and settings from the phone.

On iPhone, an older iCloud or computer backup of your whole phone may still include AutoEase’s data until that backup is replaced or deleted (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups).

Delete a to-fix note

On the To-Fix tab, tap the trash icon next to the note.

Delete your cloud backup

First turn off Back up automatically (settings → Backup) on every phone that backs up to the same account, so none of them makes a new one. Then:

  • iPhone (iCloud): iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Manage Account Storage) → AutoEase → Delete Data.
  • Android (Google Drive): open Google Drive on the web → Settings (gear) → Manage apps → AutoEase → Options → Delete hidden app data. To also remove AutoEase’s access, visit your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.

Files you exported

Backup files and CSV exports are wherever you saved or sent them. Delete them there.

Data we hold

None. AutoEase doesn’t send your vehicle data, mileage or history to us, so there’s nothing for us to delete. If you emailed support, ask us at autoease-support@lixlink.com and we’ll delete those emails.
