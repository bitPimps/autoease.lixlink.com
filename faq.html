Getting started

What is AutoEase?

AutoEase is a free car maintenance app for iPhone and Android. Scan your car’s VIN once and enter your mileage. AutoEase then shows a green, yellow or red status for each service, reminds you before something is due, and checks for open safety recalls. There’s no account and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, no login and no email address. Open the app, tap Get started and scan your car.

How long does setup take?

About a minute. Scan the VIN barcode (or type the VIN), enter your odometer reading, and choose whether to allow notifications. Then you’re on your car’s dashboard.

Which cars does AutoEase work with?

Cars, trucks and SUVs sold in the US. AutoEase identifies your car from its 17-character VIN using the U.S. government’s NHTSA vehicle database, and it uses miles and gallons.

Is AutoEase free?

Yes. Everything in AutoEase today is free, with no ads. Your first car, its reminders, recall alerts and backup will always be free.

Can I track more than one car?

Not yet. AutoEase tracks one car per phone today.

Where can I download AutoEase?

AutoEase is in testing now and is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android. This page will link to both stores as soon as it’s available.

Scanning your VIN

Where do I find my VIN?

Open the driver’s door and look at the sticker on the door jamb: it has the VIN as text and as a barcode. The VIN is also on a small plate at the bottom of the windshield on the driver’s side, and on your registration, title and insurance card. See Where to find your car’s VIN.

How do I scan my VIN?

Tap Get started, allow the camera, and point it at the VIN barcode on the sticker inside the driver’s door. Once it reads the code, AutoEase looks up your car’s year, make and model and asks for your mileage.

The scanner won’t read my VIN. What can I do?

Tap “Type it manually instead?” and enter the 17 characters. If you’d like to try the scanner again, wipe the sticker clean, find good light and hold the phone steady a few inches away. Older stickers can be too worn to scan.

Why does it say “VIN must be 17 characters”?

Every VIN since the 1981 model year has exactly 17 letters and numbers. VINs never use the letters I, O or Q, so if you see an O, type a zero.

Why does AutoEase need my camera?

Only to read the VIN barcode. AutoEase doesn’t take or keep photos. If you’d rather not allow the camera, tap Enter VIN manually.

What does AutoEase do with my VIN?

It sends the VIN to NHTSA’s free vPIC decoder to look up your car’s year, make and model, and stores it on your phone. Your VIN is never sent to us.

Your car’s status

What do green, yellow and red mean?

Green (Good) means nothing is needed. Yellow (Soon) means a service is due within 30 days or 500 miles. Red (Overdue) means it’s at or past its interval in miles or time. The big lamp on the Garage tab shows the most urgent one.

Which services does AutoEase track?

Oil change, tire rotation, brake inspection and engine air filter. Each one is checked by miles and by months, whichever comes first.

Where do the service intervals come from?

AutoEase starts with conservative intervals that suit most passenger vehicles: oil change and tire rotation every 7,500 miles or 6 months, brake inspection every 12,000 miles or 12 months, and air filter every 15,000 miles or 12 months. The oil change is adjusted for some makes: 10,000 miles or 12 months for Toyota, Ford, BMW and Mercedes-Benz, and 5,000 miles or 6 months for Nissan and Subaru. These are general intervals, not your car’s exact factory schedule, so if your owner’s manual says sooner, go by that.

I just set up the app. Why is everything green?

AutoEase starts every service as if it was just done at the mileage you entered. If you know a service is due or overdue, get it done, then tap it on the Garage tab and tap Mark as Done so its clock starts from the right point.

How do I update my mileage?

On the Garage tab, tap Update Mileage and enter the number on your odometer. Updating every couple of weeks keeps your status and mileage reminders accurate.

How do I mark a service as done?

On the Garage tab, tap the service, add what it cost if you like, and tap Mark as Done. Its clock restarts from today and your current mileage, and the job is added to your service log.

What is the on-time streak?

When you mark services done before they become overdue, AutoEase counts them. Once you have two or more on-time services in a row, a streak badge appears on the dashboard.

Reminders and notifications

When will AutoEase notify me?

About a week before a service is due by date; right away when a mileage update makes a service due soon or overdue; and, if you turned on Weather Alerts, when a freeze or extreme heat is in the forecast.

Will AutoEase send me promotional notifications?

No. Notifications are only about your car’s health. AutoEase never sends ads, offers or “we miss you” messages.

I tapped “Not now”. How do I turn notifications on?

On iPhone, go to Settings → Notifications → AutoEase and turn on Allow Notifications. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → AutoEase → Notifications. AutoEase sets up your reminders the next time you open it.

Why didn’t I get a reminder when I hit the mileage?

Your phone can’t read your odometer, so AutoEase only knows your mileage when you update it. Mileage reminders arrive when you tap Update Mileage and a service crosses into Soon or Overdue. Date-based reminders arrive on their own.

Service log and costs

What’s in the service log?

Every service you mark as done: what it was, the date, the mileage, the cost if you entered one, and whether it was done on time. The Service tab also shows your lifetime total spent.

Can I export my service history?

Yes. On the Service tab, tap the share button to export your service log as a CSV file and send it wherever you like. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, which is handy when you sell the car.

Do I have to enter a cost?

No. Cost is optional. Leave it blank if you don’t have the receipt handy.

Fuel and MPG

How do I log a fill-up?

On the Fuel tab, enter the odometer reading, the gallons you put in and, if you like, the cost.

How does AutoEase calculate MPG?

It divides the miles driven since your previous fill-up by the gallons of this fill-up. For the most accurate number, fill the tank all the way each time. Your average MPG appears once you’ve logged more than one fill-up.

Does logging a fill-up update my car’s mileage?

No. To update the mileage your service status uses, tap Update Mileage on the Garage tab.

To-fix list

What is the to-fix list?

A place to jot down small things you’ve noticed, like a squeaky brake or a rattle, so you remember them at your next service. Tap a note when it’s fixed: resolved notes move to a dated history below. Tap the trash icon to delete a note.

Safety recalls

How does AutoEase check for recalls?

When you open AutoEase, it checks the U.S. government’s NHTSA recall database for your car’s make, model and year, and shows a banner if there are any. It’s a live check, not a saved list. To check again, close AutoEase fully and reopen it.

Does a recall banner mean my car is affected?

Not always. AutoEase matches recalls by make, model and year, not by your exact VIN, so a recall might cover only certain build dates or trims. Tap the banner for details, then confirm by entering your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or calling a dealer. See How to check if your car has a safety recall.

My car’s recall was already fixed. How do I clear the banner?

Tap the recall banner and tap Mark as fixed on that recall. It moves to a Fixed section, and the banner counts only open recalls. If you marked the wrong one, tap Undo before you close the list: once no open recalls are left, the banner goes away.

Do I have to pay for a recall repair?

Usually not. Safety recall repairs are free at a dealer for your car’s brand, with limited exceptions such as vehicles more than 15 years old when the recall was announced.

Weather nudges

What are weather alerts?

An optional heads-up when the forecast near you shows a freeze (32°F or below), so you can check your tire pressure, or extreme heat (95°F or above), so you can check your coolant and battery. They’re off until you turn them on.

How do I turn weather alerts on or off?

Tap the settings (gear) button at the top of the screen and switch Weather Alerts on or off. Your phone asks for location permission the first time.

How do I clear a weather alert?

Tap the weather banner and tap Done on the alert. It moves to a Done section. To bring it back, tap Undo before you close the list: once no open alerts are left, the banner goes away.

Does AutoEase track my location?

No. With Weather Alerts on, AutoEase reads your approximate location once to fetch the forecast from Open-Meteo, then discards it. Your location is never stored or backed up.

Backup and restore

How do I back up my car’s data?

Tap the settings (gear) button, then Backup, and turn on Back up automatically. On iPhone, backups go to your own iCloud. On Android, tap Link Google Drive first. AutoEase then backs up a short while after you change something and when you leave the app.

Is backup free?

Yes, and it always will be. Losing your car’s history when a phone breaks is exactly what AutoEase is meant to prevent.

How do I restore my data?

On a new phone, tap Restore from a backup on the first screen, under Get started. You don’t need to add your car first. On Android, link the same Google account; on iPhone, use the same iCloud account. Then tap Restore from backup. When your car comes back, AutoEase asks about notifications if it hasn’t yet, then opens your Garage.

On a phone that already has AutoEase, go to settings (gear) → Backup and tap Restore from backup. Restoring adds what’s in your backup and keeps the history on this phone. If this phone has an empty copy of the same car, with nothing logged, AutoEase replaces it with the restored car.

Can I save a backup file instead?

Yes. In Backup, tap Export backup file and save it anywhere you choose, such as Files, Google Drive or email. To bring it back, tap Import a backup file. Importing adds to what’s on the phone and keeps its history.

Why does Android ask me to sign in with Google?

Google Drive needs your permission before AutoEase can save there. AutoEase asks only for its own hidden app folder in your Drive, not your other files.

Backup says iCloud isn’t available.

Make sure you’re signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is on: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive.

Privacy and your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, and in your own iCloud or Google Drive if you turn on backup. AutoEase has no accounts and no server that holds your data. On Android, if your phone’s own Google backup is on, Android may also save AutoEase’s data with the rest of your phone and bring it back when you reinstall or set up a new phone.

What information leaves my phone?

Only what a lookup needs: your VIN to NHTSA to identify your car, your car’s make, model and year to NHTSA to check recalls, and, if you turn on Weather Alerts, your approximate location to Open-Meteo for the forecast. Your mileage, service history, costs and notes never leave your phone except in a backup or file you choose to make.

Does the app track me or show ads?

No. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. This website uses Google Analytics for basic visit counts; the app doesn’t. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling AutoEase deletes everything it stored on your phone. On Android, if your phone’s own Google backup is on, Android may also save AutoEase’s data with the rest of your phone and bring it back when you reinstall or set up a new phone. A cloud backup stays in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete it there. See Delete your AutoEase data.

Troubleshooting

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Tap the settings (gear) button and choose Light, Dark or System under Display. System follows your phone’s setting.

Does AutoEase work offline?

Yes. Your dashboard, mileage updates, Mark as Done and all your logs work with no connection. Looking up a new VIN, recall and weather checks, and cloud backup need the internet; backup catches up when you’re back online.

I don’t see a recall banner. Is the check working?

No banner means AutoEase found no recalls for your car’s make, model and year, or it couldn’t connect, or you’ve marked every recall as fixed. If you were offline, close AutoEase fully and reopen it when you have a signal.

I entered the wrong mileage. How do I fix it?

On the Garage tab, tap Update Mileage and enter the correct reading. If it’s lower than the one you saved, AutoEase asks you to confirm, because odometers only count up; tap Change. Services that were counted from the wrong, higher number are counted from the corrected reading instead.
